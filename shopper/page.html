Grocery Shopper

Paste your list and click Shop. Claude Code checks prices at Walmart and Sam's Club in a Chrome window, the cheapest plan is worked out for you (fees included), and Claude fills both carts. It never checks out. You review each cart and place the orders.

First, sign in to Walmart and Sam's Club

Click the button, sign in to both stores in the Chrome window that opens (tick "Keep me signed in"), then close that window. You only need to do this once.

Fees and minimums

Used to decide whether splitting the order is worth it. Set them to match your memberships.
