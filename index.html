Split Cart

Paste your grocery list. Split Cart compares package sizes, bulk pricing, order minimums and delivery fees, then tells you what to buy where for the lowest total.

Your list

One item per line. Quantities are in the item's unit from the price book, like 2 milk (gallons), 18 eggs or 2 dozen eggs.

Fees and minimums

Set these to match your accounts. Defaults assume a Walmart order under $35 pays a $6.99 fee and a Sam's Club order under $50 pays a $12 fee. Membership and fees change, so check yours.

Price book

Split Cart can't read store prices on its own, so it uses the prices you save here. Rows marked example hold sample prices. Use the Check links to see current prices and update the row. Size is how many units come in one package, and Price is the price of one package.
